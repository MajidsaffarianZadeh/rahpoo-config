Privacy Policy — Rumi Bakery

App: com.vmap.rumibakery (Google Play) · by Vmap · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Rumi Bakery is a play-and-learn game for children aged 2 to 5: the child helps Rumi and her friends grow wheat, mill flour, knead dough, bake bread in a clay oven and share it at a picnic. We made it to be safe for small children, and we wrote this page for their parents.

In short: no ads, no accounts, no chat, no sign-in. We never ask a child for a name, a photo, a voice, a location or any other personal information. The game sends only anonymous usage and crash statistics. A grown-up can choose to send us a message; only then does anything typed by a person reach us.

What stays on the device

Kept only in the app's own storage, never sent to us, and deleted when the app is uninstalled: which chapters are done, stickers and golden stars, sound and music settings, and whether the full game has been unlocked (as Google Play reports it). We have no access to any of it.

What leaves the device

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics): game events such as "chapter 3 started", "chapter 3 completed", "free play opened" or "the purchase screen was shown", together with the app version, device model, Android version, language and a random app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for this installation. From the network connection Google derives an approximate region (country or city level). We use this only to see which parts of the game children enjoy and where they get stuck. The app does not collect the Advertising ID, does not turn on ad personalization or Google signals, and sends no name, text, picture or sound.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics): if the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report (where in the code it failed, device model, Android version, app version and a random installation identifier). No personal information.
  • "Message us" (only if a grown-up sends one): the parents' area, which sits behind a grown-ups gate, has a form for questions and ideas. It sends the message text, the kind of message, an optional e-mail address or phone number the grown-up types so we can answer, and technical context (app name and version, store, language, phone model and Android version) to our own server at nikipoo.ir, which forwards it to our support inbox. It sends nothing about the child; please do not write a child's personal details in a message. Messages are used only to answer you, are never shared or sold, and are deleted on request.
  • Purchases (only if a grown-up buys "Unlock all chapters"): payment happens entirely in Google Play's own purchase screen, which asks for the Google account's confirmation and, for a child's account in Family Link, a parent's approval. We never see card or payment details. The app receives only Google Play's confirmation that the purchase was made, and Firebase Analytics counts the purchase (product, price and currency) without any personal information.
  • Our other games: the home screen has a small "More games" shelf with our own apps. A tap opens that app if it is installed, otherwise its page in Google Play. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else by this, and no third-party ads are ever shown.

Google processes the Firebase data on our behalf under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy terms, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Firebase keeps analytics data for a limited period and then deletes it automatically. We do not share or sell any data with anyone.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, Google Play Families)

  • Rumi Bakery is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR rules for children. The statistics above are anonymous and used only for the internal operation and improvement of the game, never to contact a child, build a profile or show ads.
  • No ads of any kind, no advertising SDK, and no Advertising ID.
  • No chat, no social features, no way to talk to other people, no user-generated content that others can see.
  • The purchase, the "Message us" form, this privacy page and other settings sit in the parents' area, behind a grown-ups gate (press and hold for three seconds) that a small child is not expected to pass.
  • The game does not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, location (GPS), photos or files. Apart from internet access and the technical permissions that Google Play Billing and Firebase need to work, it requests none.

Your choices and rights

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, permanently deletes everything stored on the device; "Start over" in the parents' area clears the game's progress. Because the statistics are not linked to a name, an e-mail or an account, we cannot pick out one child's data, but you may ask us anything about them. To have a message you sent us deleted, or for any other privacy question or request (access, correction, deletion), write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the new version reaches Google Play.
